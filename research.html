Neuroscience Research

We investigate the mechanisms behind learning, memory, and neural plasticity - how experience shapes the way the brain works.

Founding and Affiliations

01 Learning and Memory

Our research explores how the brain acquires, organizes, and retrieves information, with a focus on the cognitive and neural mechanisms underlying learning and memory. We investigate how new experiences are encoded, how memories are consolidated and stored over time, and how they influence future decisions and behavior. By combining experimental methods with behavioral analysis, we examine the factors that strengthen or disrupt memory formation, including attention, repetition, and environmental context. Our work aims to deepen our understanding of how memories change over time and how the brain adapts to new information, offering insight into the fundamental processes that shape human cognition and everyday experience.
